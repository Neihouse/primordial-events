## Persona 1: Wedding Couple - "The Discerning Millennials"

### Profile

**Names**: Sarah (31) & Michael (33)
**Location**: Walnut Creek, CA
**Occupations**: Software Engineer (Sarah), Product Manager (Michael)
**Combined Income**: $250,000/year
**Wedding Budget**: $55,000 (close to Bay Area average)
**Engagement Length**: 14 months
**Wedding Date**: 8 months out

### Demographics

- **Age**: Millennials (28-43) - largest wedding demographic
- **Tech-Savvy**: Extremely comfortable with online research
- **Social Media Active**: Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok for wedding inspiration
- **Values**: Authenticity, quality, transparency, local businesses

### Quote

> "We want our wedding to feel special, not cookie-cutter. We're willing to pay more for quality, but we need to understand what we're getting. The DJ can make or break the dance floor vibe."

### Goals & Motivations

1. **Primary Goal**: Find a DJ who will keep guests dancing all night
2. **Quality Over Budget**: Premium equipment matters to them (Michael is an audiophile)
3. **Stress Reduction**: Want reliable, professional vendor who won't cause problems
4. **Personalization**: Want DJ who adapts to their music taste, not rigid playlists
5. **Visual Impact**: Lighting matters - they've seen amazing Instagram wedding videos
6. **No Regrets**: Terrified of "cheaping out" on something important

### Pain Points

1. **Information Overload**: Overwhelmed by 50+ DJ options in the Bay Area
2. **Pricing Opacity**: Frustrated by "Contact for pricing" - they have a budget
3. **Can't See Them Perform**: Hard to judge DJ skill from a website alone
4. **Hidden Fees**: Worried about surprise costs for overtime, travel, equipment
5. **Double-Booking Risk**: Heard horror stories of DJs canceling last-minute
6. **SF Travel Fees**: Don't want to pay $300 extra just because DJ is based in SF
7. **Equipment Concerns**: Don't know the difference between good and bad DJ gear

### Research Behavior

**How They Found Primordial Events**:
- **87% probability**: Google search "Walnut Creek wedding DJ" or "East Bay DJ"
- **70% probability**: The Knot vendor search
- **45% probability**: Venue preferred vendor list
- **30% probability**: Instagram hashtag #EastBayWedding
- **15% probability**: Referral from friend

**Devices**:
- **Mobile First**: 68% of first visits on iPhone (Sarah on commute, Michael during lunch)
- **Desktop Booking**: Switch to laptop for detailed research and booking form

**Search Queries**:
- "Walnut Creek wedding DJ reviews"
- "best DJ Bay Area under $4000"
- "wedding DJ with lasers East Bay"
- "Pioneer DJ equipment wedding"
- "wedding DJ prices Contra Costa County"

### User Journey on Primordial.events

#### Stage 1: Discovery (Mobile, 2-3 minutes)

**Entry Point**: Google search "Walnut Creek wedding DJ" → lands on homepage

**First 30 Seconds** (Make or Break):
- ✅ Sees "East Bay's Premium Audio Experience" - local + quality positioning
- ✅ Sees "Nightclub Sound • CDJ-3000s • BASSBOSS Speakers • No SF Travel Fees"
- ✅ Pricing visible: "Wedding packages from $2,200"
- ❓ Looks for reviews (5-star badge or review count)
- ❓ Scans for photos of real weddings

**Navigation Pattern**:
1. Homepage (30 seconds) → Quick scan
2. Services page (60 seconds) → "Premium Package $3,200 - this looks perfect"
3. Our Equipment page (45 seconds) → "Wow, they have professional gear"
4. **Bounce or Continue**: If impressed, bookmarks and continues research

#### Stage 2: Consideration (Desktop, 15-30 minutes, same day or next day)

**Return Visit**: Comes back on laptop to dig deeper

**Deep Dive Sequence**:
1. **Our Equipment** (5 min):
   - Reads CDJ-3000 specs: "32-bit audio processing - 4x better than typical DJs"
   - Sees BASSBOSS: "21Hz bass response vs 40Hz typical" - Michael is impressed
   - Comparison table convinces them of quality difference

2. **Services** (5 min):
   - Reviews all three wedding packages
   - Premium ($3,200) fits budget
   - Compares what's included vs not included
   - Checks for hidden fees

3. **Gallery** (3 min):
   - Wants to see lighting in action
   - Looks for weddings similar to their venue type
   - Sarah shares photos with Michael: "Look how good the lighting looks!"

4. **Reviews/Testimonials** (7 min):
   - Reads 5-10 reviews carefully
   - Looks for mentions of: reliability, music selection, equipment quality
   - Checks if reviews are recent (2025-2026)
   - Red flag if no reviews or all from 2020

5. **About** (2 min):
   - Wants to know who they're working with
   - Checks experience level
   - Looks for personality fit

6. **Pricing/FAQ** (3 min):
   - Looks for: overtime fees, travel fees, deposit requirements
   - Checks payment schedule
   - Reads cancellation policy

#### Stage 3: Decision (Desktop, 10-15 minutes, 1-3 days later)

**Comparison Phase**: Has 3-5 DJs shortlisted

**Primordial Events Advantages**:
- ✅ Equipment quality clearly communicated
- ✅ No SF travel fees (saves $200-300 vs competitors)
- ✅ Local to East Bay (feels more reliable)
- ✅ Pricing transparent ($3,200 vs "Contact Us")
- ✅ Premium package includes ceremony sound (saves $200 add-on)

**Conversion Triggers**:
- Fast response time (within 2 hours)
- Personalized email response (not template)
- Offer to show equipment in person or video call
- Availability on their date
- Flexible payment plan (deposit + payments vs full upfront)

**Booking Action**:
1. Fills out contact form with:
   - Wedding date, venue, guest count
   - Package interest (Premium)
   - Music preferences
   - Questions about customization
2. Expects response within 24 hours (ideally 2 hours)
3. Wants to schedule call or meeting before booking
4. May ask to see equipment demo or attend another event

### Questions They MUST Have Answered

**Before Contact**:
- ❓ What's the pricing? (Ranges shown: $2,200-$4,500)
- ❓ What equipment do you use? (CDJ-3000s, BASSBOSS - clearly explained)
- ❓ Do you charge travel fees? (No for East Bay - prominently stated)
- ❓ What's included? (Packages break down inclusions)
- ❓ Do you have good reviews? (Testimonials page + Google reviews)

**In First Contact**:
- ❓ Are you available on our date? (Availability calendar or quick response)
- ❓ Have you worked at our venue before?
- ❓ Can we see you perform or meet you first?
- ❓ What's your backup plan if equipment fails?
- ❓ How do we create our playlist / do-not-play list?
- ❓ What's your payment schedule?

**Before Signing Contract**:
- ❓ What happens if we need to reschedule? (COVID impact = top concern)
- ❓ Do you have insurance?
- ❓ Will you personally DJ our wedding or will you send someone else?
- ❓ What are overtime fees?
- ❓ How do you handle last-minute requests?

### Information Hierarchy (What Matters When)

**Immediate (First 30 seconds)**:
1. Are they local to my area?
2. Pricing ballpark
3. Quality indicators (professional vs amateur)
4. Social proof (reviews/ratings)

**Short-term (First 5 minutes)**:
1. What's included in packages
2. Equipment quality
3. Photos of real events
4. Availability

**Medium-term (15-30 minutes)**:
1. Detailed package comparisons
2. Add-ons and upgrades
3. Testimonials
4. About the DJ/company
5. Contract terms

**Long-term (Decision phase)**:
1. Personality fit
2. Music selection process
3. Timeline coordination
4. Payment terms
5. Cancellation policy

### Trust Signals That Matter

**Top 5 (in order)**:
1. **Video of DJ Performing**: Want to see crowd reaction, mixing skills
2. **Recent Reviews (2025-2026)**: Especially with photos
3. **Equipment Photos**: Professional gear = higher trust
4. **Venue Experience**: "Have you done Wente Vineyards before?"
5. **Responsive Communication**: Fast, personalized replies

**Also Important**:
- Insurance documentation
- Backup equipment guarantee
- Years in business
- Number of weddings per year
- Specific venue name-drops

### Conversion Barriers (What Makes Them Leave)

1. **No Pricing**: "Contact Us for pricing" = instant back button
2. **Slow Website**: 3+ seconds to load on mobile = bounce
3. **No Reviews**: Zero testimonials = "are they new or bad?"
4. **Outdated Content**: Last blog post from 2022 = inactive business?
5. **Generic Stock Photos**: No real event photos = unproven
6. **Complicated Navigation**: Can't find pricing/packages easily
7. **No Video**: Can't see DJ in action
8. **SF-Focused**: If they talk about SF more than East Bay
9. **Wedding-Only**: No equipment rental option for ceremony-only needs
10. **Pushy Sales**: Aggressive popups or forced phone calls

### Ideal User Experience

**Homepage** (Mobile, 30 seconds):
- Hero: "East Bay's Premium Audio Experience | Weddings from $2,200"
- Subheading: "CDJ-3000s • BASSBOSS Speakers • No SF Travel Fees"
- CTA: "View Wedding Packages" (primary) / "See Our Equipment" (secondary)
- Social proof: "4.9★ on Google (47 reviews)" or similar
- Featured equipment photo carousel
- Recent wedding photos grid

**Services Page** (Desktop, 3 minutes):
- Three wedding packages side-by-side
- "Most Popular" badge on Premium package
- Clear what's included vs add-ons
- Equipment photos for each package
- "Request Quote" CTA on each package
- FAQ section below packages

**Our Equipment Page** (Desktop, 5 minutes):
- Featured equipment grid with photos
- Comparison table (Typical DJ vs Primordial Events)
- "Why This Matters" section explaining 32-bit audio, 21Hz bass
- Video showing equipment in action (if available)
- Link to equipment rental for DIY needs

**Booking Flow** (Desktop, 5 minutes):
- Simple form: Date, Venue, Guest Count, Package Interest, Contact Info
- Optional: Music preferences, Special requests
- Expectation setting: "We'll respond within 2 hours"
- Next steps clear: "After you submit, we'll email you within 2 hours to schedule a call"

### Quote
