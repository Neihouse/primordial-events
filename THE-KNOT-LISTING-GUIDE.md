## FAQs to Add

**Q: What makes your equipment different from other DJs?**
A: We use Pioneer CDJ-3000s ($2,299 each) and BASSBOSS speakers—professional nightclub-grade equipment. Most wedding DJs use consumer gear like controllers and basic speakers. The difference is night and day in sound quality, bass response, and reliability.

**Q: Do you charge travel fees?**
A: No travel fees for East Bay locations (Martinez, Concord, Walnut Creek, Pleasant Hill, Clayton). Extended East Bay locations (Brentwood, Livermore, Dublin) are +$100. This is significantly less than San Francisco DJs who typically charge $200-300 in travel fees.

**Q: Can we request specific songs?**
A: Absolutely! We offer unlimited music requests (Ultimate package) or standard requests (all packages). We'll also do a music consultation before your event to understand your preferences and create a custom playlist.

**Q: Do you provide ceremony sound?**
A: Yes, ceremony sound is included in our Premium and Ultimate packages. We provide wireless microphones, music playback, and arrive early to set up. Add-on available for Essential package (+$200).

**Q: Can we rent your equipment without DJ services?**
A: Yes! We offer equipment-only rentals starting at $400 for basic DJ setup, $600 for premium sound system, or $1,200 for full production gear. Perfect for DIY weddings or if you have your own DJ.

**Q: How far in advance should we book?**
A: We recommend booking 6+ months in advance, especially for peak wedding season (May-October). Early booking discount: $150 off if booked 6+ months out.

**Q: What's your cancellation policy?**
A: Deposits are non-refundable but can be applied to a rescheduled date within 12 months. Full cancellation terms provided in contract.

**Q: Do you have backup equipment?**
A: Yes, we always bring backup CDJs, mixer, and microphones to every event. Our equipment is professional-grade and meticulously maintained, but we prepare for any scenario.
