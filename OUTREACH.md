## Outreach Best Practices

### Email Strategy
- **Send Time**: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm (best open rates)
- **Keep It Short**: Under 150 words for cold emails
- **Personalize**: Always include specific details about their business
- **One CTA**: One clear call-to-action per email
- **Follow Up**: 3-5 days after initial email, then 1 week later
- **Know When to Stop**: After 2-3 follow-ups, move on

### Phone Follow-Up Script (After Email)

```
"Hi [Name], this is Chance Neihouse. I sent you an email a few days ago
about [topic]. I know you're busy, but I wanted to see if you had a chance
to look at it?

[If yes]: "Great! Do you have a quick minute to chat now, or should we
schedule something?"

[If no]: "No worries! The quick version is [one-sentence pitch]. Would
you be open to a 10-minute call this week to discuss?"

[If not interested]: "I totally understand. Thanks for your time - feel
free to reach out if anything changes!"
```

### Tracking Your Outreach

Create a spreadsheet with:
- Date sent
- Contact name & business
- Email subject
- Response status (No response / Interested / Not interested / Follow up)
- Follow-up date
- Notes

### Success Metrics (90-Day Goals)

- **Venue Partnerships**: 3 preferred vendor agreements
- **Trivia Contracts**: 2 monthly recurring contracts
- **School Contacts**: 1 district on file for upcoming events
- **Planner Partnerships**: 3 active referral partners
