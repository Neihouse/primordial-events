## Step 10: Questions & Answers

**Proactively add Q&A** (helps SEO and conversions):

### Q: What makes your equipment different from other DJs?
**A**: We use Pioneer CDJ-3000s ($2,299 each) and BASSBOSS speakers - professional nightclub equipment. Most wedding DJs use consumer controllers and basic speakers. The difference is crystal-clear sound quality and bass you can feel in your chest, not just hear. Same gear used in top SF clubs, without the SF pricing.

### Q: Do you charge travel fees for East Bay events?
**A**: No travel fees for Martinez, Concord, Walnut Creek, Pleasant Hill, Clayton, Antioch, or Pittsburg. Extended East Bay locations (Brentwood, Livermore, Dublin) are only +$100. This is significantly less than San Francisco DJs who typically charge $200-300 in travel fees.

### Q: What's included in your wedding packages?
**A**: Our packages include professional DJ & MC services, Pioneer CDJ-3000 setup, BASSBOSS or RCF sound system, wireless microphones, and music consultation. Premium and Ultimate packages add intelligent lighting, laser shows, ceremony sound, and more. Packages start at $2,200 for 4 hours.

### Q: Can I rent your equipment without DJ services?
**A**: Yes! We rent professional equipment to DIY weddings, other DJs, and production companies. CDJ-3000 packages from $400/day, premium sound systems from $600/day, full production gear from $1,200/day. All rentals include setup assistance.

### Q: How far in advance should I book?
**A**: We recommend booking 6+ months in advance for weddings, especially during peak season (May-October). Corporate and school events can often be booked 2-3 months out. Early booking discount: $150 off if booked 6+ months in advance.

### Q: Do you provide ceremony sound?
**A**: Yes, ceremony sound is included in our Premium and Ultimate wedding packages. We provide wireless microphones, music playback, and arrive early for setup. Available as a $200 add-on for our Essential package.

### Q: What areas do you serve?
**A**: We're based in Martinez and primarily serve the East Bay: Martinez, Concord, Walnut Creek, Pleasant Hill, Clayton, Antioch, Pittsburg, Brentwood, Livermore, Dublin, San Ramon, and Danville. We can travel to SF or San Jose for an additional fee.

### Q: What music genres do you play?
**A**: We specialize in all genres - Electronic/EDM, Hip-Hop, Top 40, Rock, Latin, Reggaeton, Country, 80s/90s classics, and more. We'll customize the music to match your event style and take requests throughout the night.
