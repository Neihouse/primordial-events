## Executive Summary

Primordial Groove positions itself as a premium DJ and event production service in the San Francisco Bay Area, commanding higher-than-average rates justified by $50,000+ in professional-grade equipment, extensive experience, and comprehensive service offerings. Our pricing strategy emphasizes value over volume, targeting clients who prioritize quality, reliability, and a premium experience.

**Key Positioning:**
- Premium tier pricing (top 20% of Bay Area market)
- Equipment investment justifies 25-40% premium over competitors
- Focus on high-value events (weddings, corporate) vs high-volume low-margin bookings
- Recurring revenue streams (trivia, karaoke) provide stable income base

## Pricing Philosophy

### Premium Positioning vs Volume Strategy

**Our Approach: Premium Quality, Selective Volume**

We deliberately price in the premium tier for several strategic reasons:

1. **Equipment Investment Recovery:** $50K+ in professional audio, lighting, and production equipment requires premium pricing to justify ROI and ongoing maintenance costs.

2. **Client Quality:** Higher pricing attracts serious clients who value professionalism, reducing cancellations, difficult clients, and payment issues.

3. **Time Investment:** Each event requires 20-30 hours of preparation (client meetings, playlist curation, venue coordination, setup/teardown) beyond the event itself.

4. **Insurance & Licensing:** Commercial liability insurance, music licensing (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC), and business overhead costs.

5. **Expertise Premium:** Years of experience in reading crowds, technical troubleshooting, and seamless event execution.

**Target Client Profile:**
- Weddings: Budget-conscious to luxury ($30K-100K+ total wedding budget)
- Corporate: Mid-size to enterprise companies with professional event budgets
- Schools: Well-funded districts and private schools
- Recurring: Established venues and restaurants with consistent entertainment needs

## Payment Terms

### Standard Payment Structure

#### Weddings & Private Events

**Deposit:** 25% non-refundable deposit to secure date
- Due at contract signing
- Holds date exclusively
- Applied to final balance

**Second Payment:** 50% due 30 days before event
- Required to finalize event details
- Confirms equipment allocation
- Timeline and playlist lock-in

**Final Payment:** 25% due 7 days before event
- Must be paid before equipment leaves for event
- No day-of-event payments accepted
- Late payment: $50/day fee

**Accepted Payment Methods:**
- Bank transfer (ACH) - preferred
- Credit card (+3% processing fee)
- Check (must clear 10 days before event)
- Venmo/PayPal for deposits under $500

---

#### Corporate Events

**NET 30 Terms Available:**
- For established companies with verified tax ID
- Invoice sent immediately after event
- Payment due within 30 days
- Late payment: 1.5% monthly interest

**Deposit Required:**
- 50% deposit for first-time corporate clients
- Waived for companies with established relationship (3+ events)

**Purchase Orders:**
- Accepted from corporations
- Must be received 14 days before event
- PO number required on all communications

---

#### Recurring Services (Trivia, Karaoke)

**Monthly Invoicing:**
- Invoice sent 1st of each month
- Payment due by 15th of month
- Covers all events for that month

**Quarterly Pre-Payment Option:**
- Pay 3 months in advance
- Receive 5% discount
- Locks in rate (no price increases during period)

**Annual Pre-Payment Option:**
- Pay 12 months in advance
- Receive 10% discount
- Rate guaranteed for full year
- Preferred scheduling priority

### Cancellation Policy

#### Weddings & Private Events

**More than 180 days before event:**
- Full refund minus $250 administrative fee
- Rescheduling to another date (within 18 months): no fee

**90-180 days before event:**
- 50% refund of total contract value
- Rescheduling: $150 fee

**30-90 days before event:**
- 25% refund of total contract value
- Rescheduling: $300 fee

**Less than 30 days before event:**
- No refund
- Deposit and all payments forfeited
- Rescheduling: 50% of original contract value as rescheduling fee

**Weather/Venue Cancellations:**
- Full rescheduling within 18 months at no additional cost
- Subject to availability
- Must provide venue cancellation documentation

---

#### Corporate Events

**More than 60 days before event:**
- Full refund minus 10% administrative fee
- Free rescheduling

**30-60 days before event:**
- 50% refund
- Rescheduling: $200 fee

**Less than 30 days before event:**
- No refund
- Rescheduling: 50% of contract value

---

#### Recurring Services

**Cancellation Notice:**
- 30 days written notice required
- Final month must be paid in full
- Early termination: remainder of contract term at 50%

**Venue Closure/Force Majeure:**
- Contract terminates immediately
- Refund of any pre-paid future events

---

### Late Payment Consequences

**7-14 days late:**
- Friendly reminder email
- No penalties (one-time courtesy)

**15-30 days late:**
- $50 late fee applied
- Phone call/formal notice
- Future bookings require payment in full upfront

**30+ days late:**
- $100 late fee applied
- 1.5% monthly interest on balance
- Account sent to collections after 60 days
- Legal action for amounts over $2,000
